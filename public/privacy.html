Sleep Reset — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

What data we collect

Sleep Reset collects only the data you explicitly provide:

  • Your Google account email (used for authentication via Supabase OAuth).
  • Sleep logs you record: bedtime timestamps, wake-time timestamps, and optional notes.
  • Your sleep goal configuration (target bedtime, target wake time, melatonin preferences).

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and associate your logs with your account.
  • Display your sleep history and compute personalized gradual-shift recommendations.

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party for advertising or analytics purposes.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database hosted on secure infrastructure. Your sleep logs are accessible only to your authenticated account.

Local storage

The extension stores your Supabase session token in chrome.storage.local so you stay logged in between popup opens. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions

  • storage — to persist your session and settings locally.
  • identity — to initiate Google OAuth login via the Chrome identity API.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of all your data by contacting us at fmarcosdev@gmail.com. We will delete your account and all associated sleep logs within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
